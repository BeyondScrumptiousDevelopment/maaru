**Day 1 — 16:20.** Arrived on the bus from Bhuj. Everyone off at once, everyone with too many bags. The town smells of sea and diesel and fried things.

**Day 2 — 06:50.** Boatyard. The dhows are built by eye: no drawings that I could see, just men on scaffolding made of branches, fitting planks that have been shaped by hand. One of them asked if I wanted to buy a boat. I said not today.

**Day 3 — 13:00.** Too hot to do anything, so did nothing, on a wall, in the shade, for two hours. Watched a goat try and fail to get into a shop.

**Day 4 — 19:30.** The beach at dusk is a whole town out at once. Camel rides, corn on a stick, a man selling balloons shaped like cartoon characters that have been lightly altered for copyright reasons.

**Day 5 — 08:00.** Bus back. Sand in everything.
