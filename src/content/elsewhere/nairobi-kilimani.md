**Day 1 — 22:30.** Landed. The air at night smells of rain and charcoal even when it has not rained.

**Day 3 — 07:10.** Watched the flats across the road wake up. Curtains, radios, someone doing press-ups on a balcony with a lot of commitment.

**Day 6 — 16:00.** Rain, right on time. Twenty minutes of everything. Then the road steams and the boda bodas come back like nothing happened.

**Day 9 — 12:40.** River Road. A record shop up a flight of stairs that you would never find unless someone took you. Benga, rumba, old Kenyan pressings in sleeves soft as cloth. Bought three, could have bought thirty.

**Day 14 — 05:30.** Taxi to the airport in the dark. The driver had the radio on a station playing house music, and for a minute it felt like the 140.
