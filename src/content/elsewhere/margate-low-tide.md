**10:20.** Train from Victoria. Everyone else on the train seems to be going to the same exhibition.

**12:00.** Low tide. The sea goes out so far you could walk to France, or at least it feels like it. The light is flat and silver and very kind to concrete.

**15:30.** Arlington House: the one tall block on the seafront, like someone put a piece of the city down on the beach by mistake. I love it.

**18:45.** Train back. Fridge magnet acquired.
