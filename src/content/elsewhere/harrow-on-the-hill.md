**Day 1 — 07:40.** Decided to spend a Sunday walking around Harrow like I had never been here. Bought a coffee I would not normally buy. Read the blue plaques.

**09:15.** Up the hill. It is steeper than I remember from school, or I am older. The view from the churchyard goes all the way to the reservoirs, and on a clear day further.

**11:30.** Down the other side. Lowlands Road has the best shadows at this time of year, long ones, cut by the railings.

**14:00.** Back on the high street, which I know too well to see. Tried anyway. Noticed that the old bank is now a gym, a café and a dentist, all at once, in different floors.

**17:50.** Home. Twelve thousand steps in a place I already live. Felt, briefly, like somewhere new.
