**11:00.** Forty-one degrees on the street. Twenty-something at the bottom of the stepwell. Architecture as air conditioning, worked out centuries before anyone had the word.

**11:20.** Sat on the lowest dry step and listened. Pigeons, the city a long way up, my own breathing. Someone had scratched a name and a date into the stone, 1987, which made me think about the tiffin.

**12:10.** Back up into the heat, slowly.
