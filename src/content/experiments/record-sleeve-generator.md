Real album covers belong to other people. So every frequency on this site gets a sleeve generated from its own details: the catalogue number picks a colour field and the position of a big circle; the title is set in Cormorant; the grain is the same grain as everywhere else.

It means a new entry looks finished the moment it is published, with nothing to design. Every sleeve is different and every sleeve is related, which is roughly what a record label is.

Have a look at [Frequencies](/frequencies/) to see them in the crate.
