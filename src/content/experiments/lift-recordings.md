Every lift has a voice, even the ones that do not talk. The doors have a rhythm, the motor has a pitch, the chime is either one note or two. The hospital lift says *doors closing* in a voice so tired you want to give it a holiday.

Twelve lifts recorded so far. Paused, because the one I actually want — the car park lift, out of order since 2019 — is still out of order.
