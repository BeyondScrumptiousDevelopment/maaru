The plan was simple: one sheet of A4, folded into eight pages with one cut, photocopied at the library, left on the bus. A new issue every week.

I made three. Then I missed a week, then another, and that was that. Abandoned is fine. The three issues exist. Somebody on the 140 might still have one.

What I learned: weekly is too often for anything you want to be good. The photocopier at the library adds more grain than any filter.
