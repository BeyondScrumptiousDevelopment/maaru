Some music sounds like rooms. Dub techno sounds like the top deck of the multi-storey at six o'clock: wide, grey, wet, mostly echo. The chord hits and then spends a long time leaving.

Good for walking. Bad for anything that needs concentration. Perfect for staring at concrete.
