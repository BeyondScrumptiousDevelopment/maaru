A Sunday-night selection that starts in Chicago and ends somewhere much quieter. The order matters more than the tracks. It should feel like the room slowly emptying.

Side B is for when the lights are already off. The last one is not house music at all, and that is on purpose: Ayub Ogada's voice and a nyatiti is the right way to close a door.
